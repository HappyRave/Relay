Relay's website: round W2, what Relay can do

The brief, 2026-11-22: "show more about relay, more about its capabilities". Today the landing page shows six feature cards; these four variants replace that section, between the editor screenshot and the download band (the striped bars mark what stays). They cover what 2.0 adds: Show targets, Every…, Activity, Text and data files, AutoHotkey export. Screenshots are the guide's own (docs/images); Show targets is drawn, since screenshots can't show its marks.

Jobs

Claude's pickOpen full page ↗

Capabilities shown as chores people hand Relay: fill 200 forms from a spreadsheet, click OK when the dialog appears, open your tools every morning, act when a status turns red, send a colleague a program, do it 50 times, sign off an email with one key. Each card is a few real-looking steps and what it uses; Show targets gets a wide card. Then And everything else, a dense two-column list so nothing is missing.

Why: people come with a chore, not a feature name, and a steps list shows how Relay thinks in one look, like the hero already does. The closing list keeps it as complete as A or D.

Round W1

Decided, 2026-11-21

The maintainer chose W1·A Live desk for the landing page and W1·G1 Three columns for the guide: "A and G1". Then decided the same day: the headline "Do it once. Relay does it again."; light and dark themes, following the system; links into the spec go to GitHub; the site is at happyrave.github.io/Relay. Still a proposal: the rest of A's copy.

Four landing pages (A–D) and two guide pages (G1, G2). Each is a different idea, built from the app's own tokens: aluminium and ink, red only for record and download, Archivo, the step glyphs (blue pointer, yellow keys, green logic). The frames are live: scroll inside them, and try the interactive heroes. Each opens full size from its link.

W1·C

The page is a macro

Claude's pickOpen full page ↗

Scrolling plays the page. A steps list on the left (Record, Edit, Wait for, Trigger, Play) lights the step you're on; a timeline docked at the bottom moves its red playhead as you scroll, with Download always one click away. Each step is a section with a real screenshot. On a phone, the list folds away and the timeline stays.

Why: it explains Relay by being Relay: the steps and the playhead are the product's own ideas, so the page teaches the editor before anyone downloads it. And it's the most memorable without being a gimmick.
