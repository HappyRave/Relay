Record a macro

Record a task so that it plays back right the first time.

Find it Record in the Library, or Ctrl Shift F9
WarningRelay doesn't record clicks on its own windows, such as the player. Move the player away from where you click first.

Start and stop a recording

To record, press the record key, do the task, then press Esc.

  1. Open the app to record.
  2. Select Record, or press the record key. Relay's window hides and the player shows.
  3. The player counts 3, 2, 1. When it shows REC, do the task.
  4. Press Esc or the record key, or select Stop on the player.
  5. Type a name. It replaces the default name, such as Recording 1.
✓ If it worked, Relay says Recording stopped, … steps. Name this macro.

More detail: Recording: starting and stopping

What Relay records, and what it doesn't

Relay records clicks, keys, scrolls, mouse moves and the time between them.
