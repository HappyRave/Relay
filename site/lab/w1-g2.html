Record a macro

Record a task so that it plays back right the first time.

⚠Relay doesn't record clicks on its own windows. Move the player away from where you click first.
Find itRecord or Ctrl Shift F9
1

Open the app to record.

2

Select Record, or press the record key. Relay's window hides and the player shows.

3

The player counts 3, 2, 1. When it shows REC, do the task.

4

Press Esc or the record key, or select Stop on the player.

5

Type a name. It replaces the default name, such as Recording 1.

If it worked, Relay says Recording stopped, … steps. Name this macro.
What Relay records, and what it doesn't
Relay records clicks, keys, scrolls, mouse moves and the time between them. It doesn't record the record key, Esc, or an app that runs as administrator.
Record the Esc key
Turn off Esc stops recording in Settings, then stop with the record key.
Good to know
Relay keeps what you type as plain text. Don't record a password.
